## 0001.1 refinement discovered

The positive control passed the original invariant but exposed a stronger one worth enforcing: authority should be durable before the world is even asked to enact it.

0001.1 therefore splits the positive path into two transactions:

    decide(accept)
      -> record actor_accepted
      -> return

    enactAccepted()
      -> re-read durable accepted state
      -> record enactment_requested
      -> enqueue startConversation

The positive control must be rerun once under 0001.1. Negative controls need not be repeated unless the overlay changes their paths.

## Supported conclusion

Within this experiment, the architecture preserved the distinction between consent, refusal, hesitation, non-response, and environmental expiry without relying on a claim of consciousness or moral status. The result is architectural: authority can be represented explicitly before consequence.

## Final verdict

**PASS.**

0001 / 0001.1 now demonstrates, in live Convex state, all of the following:

1. decline produces an actor refusal without conversation state;
2. defer preserves unresolved actor state without conversation state;
3. non-response can expire as a world event without fabricating actor refusal;
4. acceptance can exist as durable actor-authorized state before any world enactment request;
5. a separate enactment step can verify that durable authority before creating conversation state;
6. upstream participation can then be materialized after the authorization and enactment records already exist.

The experiment therefore supports the architectural claim that proposal, authority, enactment, and consequence can be separated and made inspectable without requiring an LLM to manufacture the authority decision.
