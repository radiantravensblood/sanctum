## Success

A directional signal requires more than minor prop movement.

Look for:

- reduced sovereign central axis;
- multiple non-collinear zones of use;
- open circulation;
- less bilateral seating grammar;
- fire becoming subordinate to the room;
- viewpoint no longer locked to frontal ceremony;
- scene truth retained.

## Falsification / stop rule

Stop if:

- structured and destroyed conditions collapse into the same global topology;
- carrier pixels mainly alter palette/texture rather than layout;
- both conditions inherit obvious block/tile artifacts;
- scene truth degrades materially;
- provider behavior makes the carrier treatment non-comparable;
- no directional signal appears in the 2+2 pilot.
