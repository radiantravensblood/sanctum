## Synthesis

Kernel 0039 succeeds at **preservation** and only partially succeeds at **place-identity expression**.

The new layer does not collapse the painterly hand, flatten the four seed basins into one composition, or destabilize authored scene truth. This is a meaningful architectural success: Place Memory can coexist with seed morphology and established visual treatment.

However, the intervention is underpowered. Its visible effects are mostly micro-variation, modest object-density changes, and slight shifts toward inhabitation. It does not reliably dislodge the inherited visual prior of a dark historical parlor organized around a fireplace.

The failure mode is therefore **under-expression, not chaos**.

## Interpretation

0039 should be retained as a stable candidate-local carrier.

The next kernel should not simply add more adjectives or stronger generic prompting. It should test whether **visual place memory** can carry world vocabulary without donating a reference scene's exact architecture, furniture placement, composition, people, or object inventory.
